## Highlights

Adds dynamic tournament stage classification, elimination-aware bracket sizing, live qualifier controls, and race timing announcer callouts, plus a Hub-and-Spoke setup diagram in `README.md`.

## New Features & Improvements

- **Dynamic stage classification & bracket sizing**: Automatically sizes single-elimination and double-elimination round graphs from the active pilot count or upstream advancing qualifiers.
- **Qualifier controls & timing announcer**: Adds live qualifier round controls and spoken race/heat callouts alongside pilot lap announcements.
- **Hub & Spoke setup diagram**: Adds a visual hardware topology diagram to `README.md`.

## Bug Fixes

- **Root round seat channels**: Preserves manual seat channel assignments (`R1`, `R3`, `R5`, `R8`, etc.) on empty root rounds when connecting downstream bracket edges.

## Downloads & Firmware Compatibility

- **App binaries**: Single-file executables for Windows (`amd64`), macOS (`arm64` & `amd64`), and Linux (`amd64` & `arm64`).
- **XIAO ESP32-C5 firmware**: `slaprf-xiao-c5.bin` (`v0.1.7`, wire protocol `v2`, no reflash required from `v0.0.4`).

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/kaack/SlapRF/compare/v0.0.4...v0.0.5
