### ✨ What's New

| Feature | What Changed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AI Crossing Detector (`SlapRF Base`)** | Fly immediately at `25 mW` to `200 mW+` with the pre-trained `SlapRF Base (1P)` neural detector, or switch to traditional **DSP** `Enter` and `Exit` dBm thresholds in **Track**. |
| **On-Device AI Training & Fine-Tuning** | Fine-tune `SlapRF Base` or train custom models in **Practice -> AI Training**, compare **DSP vs. AI** accuracy scorecards, and export or import `.tar.gz` bundles. |
| **Practice Waveform Review & Auto-Label** | Edit recorded `.rfbin` waveforms lap by lap in **Practice -> Review**, split battery-swap gaps with **Break**, and auto-label passes on desktop. |
| **Signals Crossing Editor** | Unlock **Signals** charts to add, drag, or remove crossings, drag threshold pills, and view `OOO`, `SHORT`, `LONG`, and `NOISE` filter codes. |

---

### 🛠️ Improvements

- **Sequential Gate Order** · Require **Track** gates in flight order and label Start/Finish crossings with `L<n>` tags.
- **Crossing Beeps** · Simplify **AI** mode crossing beeps to `Off | On` with clearer `150 ms` tones.
- **Practice Time Breaks** · Render zigzag time-break rows between battery packs in **Laps** and **Review** tables.
- **Network Sample Counter** · Display live received sample counts per connected timer on **Network** cards.

---

### 🩹 Bug Fixes

- **Android Crossing Audio** · Play every crossing beep without delay on Android and capture tones in screen recordings.
- **Start-Gate Takeoffs** · Detect first-pass start-gate takeoffs reliably after arming or idling on the line.
- **Practice Review Traces** · Load recorded RSSI waveforms immediately in **Review** after pausing an active session.
- **Timer Fingerprint Checks** · Verify timer hardware fingerprints across reboots and slot changes to prevent mixed telemetry.
