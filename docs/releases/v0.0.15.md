### ✨ What's New

| Feature | What Changed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Draggable Crossing Lines** | Pause **Signals**, select a timer, and drag any crossing line left or right to adjust lap and section times. |
| **Live In-Progress Lap Row** | Watch the active lap row fill in section splits as you fly, hold on finish, and keep full lap history. |
| **4-Mode Crossing Beep** | Choose **Off**, **Enter**, **Peak**, or **Exit** crossing beeps per timer in **Track** and **Signals**. |
| **Two-Step Lap Cleanup** | Scratch a **Practice** lap to reveal a delete icon, or clear all laps for one pilot. |

---

### 🛠️ Improvements

- **Mobile Practice Laps** · Centered KPI strip with live lap clock, **Clear Laps** button, and steady card height.
- **Wide Track Courses** · Sideways-scrolling section columns with left and right chevrons when tracks exceed 3 sections.
- **Signals Crossing Labels** · Crossing markers show the counted lap (`H1 · Lap 2`, `T1 · Lap 2`) instead of estimated speed.
- **Unified Fastest Pills** · Fastest sections and fastest total lap times share the same neon highlight pill.

---

### 🩹 Bug Fixes

- **Shallow Jitter Rejection** · Rejects brief threshold-edge RSSI jitter using area above the enter line, and locks stronger peaks.
- **Desktop Track Rules** · Fixed dropped clicks, border flashing, and per-timer rule override inheritance in **Track**.
