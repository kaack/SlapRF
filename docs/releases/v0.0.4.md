## Highlights

Upgrades XIAO ESP32-C5 firmware (`v0.1.7`) to `500 Hz` on-chip multi-pass `5.8 GHz` sweeps with an IRAM-resident periodic power meter for lower noise variance and faster channel hopping.

## New Features & Improvements

- **`500 Hz` multi-pass `5.8 GHz` sweep (`v0.1.7`)**: Executes 5 RF passes per `10 ms` tick and combines them via a trimmed linear mean into `100 Hz` `0.5 dB` samples, rejecting single-pass `5 GHz` Wi-Fi burst outliers on chip.
- **IRAM single-pass periodic meter**: Moves the inner ADC IQ band-power loop into IRAM to eliminate flash cache stalls and tighten sweep duration consistency.
- **Hardware setup documentation**: Clarifies Hub USB powering, battery-powered Spoke placement, and single-board flashing in `README.md`.

## Downloads & Firmware Compatibility

- **App binaries**: Single-file executables for Windows (`amd64`), macOS (`arm64` & `amd64`), and Linux (`amd64` & `arm64`).
- **XIAO ESP32-C5 firmware**: `slaprf-xiao-c5.bin` (`v0.1.7`, wire protocol `v2`; compatible with `v0.0.2`+ wire format, reflash recommended to gain `500 Hz` multi-pass Wi-Fi rejection).
