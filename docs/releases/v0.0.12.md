## Highlights

Fixes Linux Bluetooth LE Hub discovery and connection on hosts where BlueZ assigns a non-`hci0` controller index (such as `hci1` on multi-adapter PCs, USB Bluetooth dongles, or re-enumerated controllers), and hardens BlueZ GATT scan/notification cleanup.

## Bug Fixes

- **Linux BlueZ dynamic adapter selection (`slaprf_ble`)**: Replaced `tinygo.org/x/bluetooth`'s hardcoded `hci0` adapter with D-Bus `org.bluez.Adapter1` discovery (preferring the first `Powered: true` controller in natural `hci0`, `hci1`, ... order, with `/sys/class/bluetooth/hci*` fallback and `SLAPRF_BLE_ADAPTER` environment override), resolving `adapter /org/bluez/hci0 does not exist` errors on `slaprf-linux-amd64` and `slaprf-linux-arm64`.
- **Linux BLE scan stop race**: Guarded per-scan `StopScan()` calls with `sync.Once` so scan-window expiration timers and pre-connect scan teardown cannot race and panic on a double-closed channel.
- **Linux GATT notification teardown**: Disabled characteristic notifications (`EnableNotifications(nil)`) before disconnecting on Linux so BlueZ D-Bus `PropertiesChanged` signal listeners and reader goroutines do not accumulate across reconnects.

## Improvements

- **Linux USB & Bluetooth setup guide**: Documented the XIAO ESP32-C5 (`303a:1001`) `uaccess` `udev` rule for immediate `/dev/ttyACM*` access without logging out, plus `bluetoothctl` and `SLAPRF_BLE_ADAPTER` troubleshooting steps.

## Downloads & Firmware Compatibility

- **App binaries**: Single-file executables for Windows (`amd64`), macOS (`arm64` & `amd64`), Linux (`amd64` & `arm64`), and Android (`arm64-v8a` APK).
- **XIAO ESP32-C5 firmware**: `slaprf-xiao-c5.bin` (USB Hub & Spokes) and `slaprf-xiao-c5-ble.bin` (Bluetooth Hub), wire protocol `v2` (compatible with `v0.0.8` through `v0.0.11` timers, no reflash required).
