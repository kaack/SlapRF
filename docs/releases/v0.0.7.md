## Highlights

Introduces the native **SlapRF Android app** (`arm64-v8a` APK with USB-C OTG and Bluetooth LE support), **SlapRF Bluetooth Hub mode** (`slaprf-xiao-c5-ble.bin`) with multi-gate `2.4 GHz` ESP-NOW Spoke collection, a 3-tier per-timer/per-pilot **Signals Fine-Tune** drawer with zero-latency crossing beeps, and learned pass-profile `5 GHz` Wi-Fi spike rejection.

## New Features

- **Native Android app (`slaprf-android-arm64.apk`)**: Runs the full Go timing engine, `bbolt` database, USB serial host (`XIAO ESP32-C5` & `RotorHazard USB`), and Bluetooth LE GATT client natively on Android phones and tablets with no external server required.
- **SlapRF Bluetooth Hub firmware (`slaprf-xiao-c5-ble.bin`)**: Connects a battery-powered Hub (`H1`) wirelessly over Bluetooth LE (`2M PHY`, phase-locked `10 ms` intervals, Claim-on-Pair `AUTH` lock, and 3-second `BOOT` pairing reset) while collecting `S1`/`S2`/`S3` splits from wireless ESP-NOW Spokes (`T1`-`T6`).
- **3-tier Signals Fine-Tune drawer & crossing beep**: Tune `Minimum lap time`, `Maximum lap time`, `Minimum crossing time`, and zero-latency synthetic `Beep on crossing` globally, per timer, or per pilot+timer, with optional retroactive practice replay and a slide-up bottom sheet on mobile.
- **RotorHazard USB spectrum scan & `26 Hz` polling**: Adds progressive live `5180`-`5945 MHz` spectrum scan and waterfall support (covering `R8` `5917 MHz`) on `0`-`255` RSSI scale for direct USB RotorHazard nodes.

## Improvements & Bug Fixes

- **`5 GHz` Wi-Fi spike rejection & `15 min` RSSI history**: Rejects narrow RF bursts using hold-time, curve area, and learned pass-profile checks (rolling back lockouts on rejected spikes), improves multi-pack auto-calibration, and extends in-memory signal scrollback to `15 minutes`.
- **Zero-stutter status & zoomed RSSI playhead**: Eliminates `1 Hz` status broadcast stutters and zoomed-in playhead velocity whips, and adds 2-finger vertical page scrolling over charts on touch screens.
- **BLE + ESP-NOW radio coexistence**: Widens `2.4 GHz` duty cycle at `50 Hz`, matches the 34-byte `spoke_join_frame_t` struct across USB Spokes and BLE Hubs, and releases Debug/Network survey streams automatically when navigating away or arming a race.

## Downloads & Firmware Compatibility

- **App binaries**: Single-file executables for Windows (`amd64`), macOS (`arm64` & `amd64`), Linux (`amd64` & `arm64`), and Android (`slaprf-android-arm64.apk`).
- **XIAO ESP32-C5 firmware**:
  - `slaprf-xiao-c5.bin`: USB Hub & all wireless Spoke timers (`T1`-`T6`), wire protocol `v2`.
  - `slaprf-xiao-c5-ble.bin`: Dedicated wireless Bluetooth Hub image (`H1`), wire protocol `v2`.
