### ✨ What's New

| Feature | What Changed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **PoE Ethernet Bridge (`SlapRF Network`)** | Place Hub `H1` up to `100 m` away over one `802.3af` PoE Ethernet cable using a Waveshare `ESP32-P4-WIFI6-POE-ETH` bridge, with no battery needed at the gate. |
| **PoE Bridge Control Screen** | Connect in **Network** to unlock the **PoE Bridge** screen for live Ethernet link status, DHCP or static IPv4 setup, USB-A baud rate, attached timer restart, and remote bridge reboot. |
| **PoE Bridge Firmware Flasher** | Choose **Gate Timer** or **PoE Bridge** in Step 1 of **Flasher** (and the online docs flasher) to flash `slaprf-p4-poe-bridge.bin` over USB-C in about 20 seconds. |
| **PoE Hardware Setup Guide** | Follow the new **PoE Ethernet Bridge** documentation guide with a complete bill of materials, wiring diagram, and embedded browser flasher. |

---

### 🛠️ Improvements

- **LAN Bridge Discovery** · Find PoE Bridges across your local network in **Network -> Discover** within a fraction of a second.
- **Exclusive Serial Session** · Protect active timing sessions on a PoE Bridge from accidental second client connections.
- **Network Connect Controls** · Switch **Connect** to **Disconnect** alongside **Discover** while linked to a PoE Bridge.
- **Documentation & README** · Highlight SlapRF DIY timers and PoE Bridge setups first, with **LapRF 8-Way** and **RotorHazard** grouped in an external hardware section.

---

> [!TIP]
> **No timer reflash needed** · Existing `v0.0.8+` XIAO ESP32-C5 USB Hub (`H1`) and Spoke (`T1`-`T6`) timers work directly with `v0.0.18` and the PoE Bridge.
